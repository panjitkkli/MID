Gunpla isn t just plastic model kits; it s a 'safe haven' in a busy world. Each snip from the runner, every part sanded and put together, is a moment of pure mindfulness where I can disconnect from stress and just be with myself. Watching it all come together into an awesome mobile suit, and feeling that pride when it sits on my desk, is truly healing—plus, it's a blank canvas for my own creativity.
